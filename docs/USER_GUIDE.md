# User guide

## Signing in
Use your username and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app if two-factor sign-in
is on. After 5 wrong attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes; a partner can unlock it under
**Users → Reset 2FA / unlock**. Forgot your password? Use *Forgot your password?* on the sign-in page.
**My account** (your name, top right) is where you change your password and turn on two-factor sign-in.

## Daily work

| I want to… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Find anything | The search bar at the top: client names, file numbers, case numbers, fee notes. |
| Add a client | **Clients → New client** |
| Open a file | **Matters → New matter** (or *New matter* on the client's page). A checklist for the matter type is added automatically. |

| Record time | **+ New → Time entry**, or Matter → **Time**. Enter hours (1.5) or minutes. |

| Upload a document | Matter → **Documents → Upload** |
| Generate a letter | Matter → **Documents → Generate from precedent** |

## Billing
1. Matter → **Actions → Draft fee note**. Unbilled time and disbursements are pre-selected; add a fixed fee if needed.
2. Check the draft; remove lines with the ✕ if necessary; then **Issue fee note**. Issued fee notes can't be edited – void and redraft instead (voiding returns the time to "unbilled").
3. **Email** sends it to the client as a PDF; **PDF** / **Print** for paper.
4. When paid: **Record payment**, or **Pay from trust** if the money is in trust.

## Trust account (partners and bookkeeper)
- **Receipt** when money comes in for a client; print the trust receipt.
- **Payment** when paying out for a client. The system **refuses any payment that would take a matter's
  trust balance below zero** – one client's money can never be used for another.
- **Transfer to matter** moves money between two matters' ledgers.
- **Pay from trust** (on a fee note) moves fees to the business account.
- Mistakes are corrected with **Reverse**, which posts an equal and opposite entry. Nothing is deleted.
- **Month end**: Trust account → **Reconcile**. Pick the bank statement date, tick the entries on the
  statement, enter the statement balance, save. The reconciliation must show *Balanced*: bank statement =
  cash book = total of all matter balances.

## Business account and VAT (partners and bookkeeper)
Fee payments appear automatically. Record other income and every expense (**Expense**, tick *Amount
includes VAT* when you have a valid tax invoice). **Reports → VAT report** gives the figures for your VAT
return; **Income statement** shows profit for any period.

## Client statements
Client page → **Statement** → PDF or **Email to client**.
